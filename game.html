PHYSICS IS THE GAME

REAL PHYSICS. REAL POSSIBILITIES.

NO SCRIPTED SOLUTIONS.

Objects have mass. Materials react differently. Gravity matters. Every impact can change the outcome.

Physics Gravity · Mass · Speed
Materials Friction · Restitution · Resistance
Environment Structures · Terrain · Objects
Enemies Mass · Health · Movement
Feedback Particles · Camera · Sound · Haptics

06

LUMEN

A VIRTUAL CURRENCY FOR GAMEPLAY.

LUMEN

PLAY. COLLECT. CUSTOMIZE.

LUMEN are a virtual currency earned through gameplay. They are used to unlock cosmetics, decorations and progression content.

✓ CAN

  • Earn through gameplay
  • Unlock cosmetics
  • Unlock decorations
  • Support progression
  • Reward engagement

× CANNOT

  • Have monetary value
  • Be converted to LUNC
  • Be withdrawn
  • Be transferred
  • Create pay-to-win
